Zone, The Focus Room

One room, one reading, one honest look at how your attention moves.

The visitor experience, designed as code. A guest sits down, puts in one earbud, and reads, while the room watches their focus rise, break, and recover. These are the surfaces, in the order a guest meets them.

00 · FoundationsThe shared source of truthBoth the design and the build read from these two files.
Design tokens
tokens.css

The single source of truth, imports the bound Zone Design System token sheet and aliases its variables (the focus line is the DS's In Flow gold). Intentional departures for this room are documented inline.

The line language
lib/focusline.js

The focus-line generator and the four archetype shapes. The live line, the reveal, the card, the profile and the email all draw from it, so the shape is identical everywhere.

Display type is a neutral grotesque stand-in for PP Neue Montreal, which is bundled at build. Dynamic content, the archetype, the caption, the four reads, the focus-line path, the matched door, is marked as named slots and placeholders throughout.
